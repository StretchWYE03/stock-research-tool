# Privacy Policy

**Ticker - Stock Research Terminal**

*Last updated: August 7, 2026*

Ticker is designed around one principle: **your data stays on your device.**
This policy describes what little data the software touches and how it is handled.

## What we collect

**Nothing that identifies you.** Ticker has no accounts, no sign-ups, no
tracking pixels, and no analytics. It does not collect your name, email, IP
address history, location, banking information, or trading history.

## Market data

Ticker fetches **public, non-personal market data** (prices, history,
fundamentals, news, options) from third-party public feeds (e.g., Yahoo
Finance's public endpoints). This data is delivered on demand and cached
temporarily in memory to stay within rate limits. The market data itself may
be delayed and is provided "as is."

## Local storage

Some features are stored **only in your browser's local storage** and never
leave your device:

- Your **watchlist**
- Your **paper-trading portfolio** and trade history
- UI preferences (theme, consent to the disclaimer)

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of the above.

## What we don't do

- We do not sell or share personal information (we have none).
- We do not serve ads or embed third-party trackers.
- We do not transmit your watchlist or portfolio anywhere.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on the project repository.
